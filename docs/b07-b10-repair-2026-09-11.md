## B07 — wavelets

- [WaveletPacket.cs](../sources/Wavelet/WaveletPacket.cs) builds the Bior/CDF
  synthesis filters from the opposite analysis branch with alternating signs.
  Independent reversal was valid for orthogonal banks, but did not provide the
  dual filters required here. Padding gives all four filters equal even lengths
  while preserving the periodic analysis origin. Synthesis gain accounts for
  the high-pass sign convention and downsampling.
- The existing [Bior table](../sources/Wavelet/Discrete/WaveletPacket.Bior.cs)
  now supplies the missing padding in Bior28. The existing
  [CDF table](../sources/Wavelet/Discrete/WaveletPacket.CDF.cs) corrects CDF53's
  high-pass coefficients. Its old sum was approximately -3.5355; the repaired
  filter has zero mean and three vanishing moments. A round trip alone did not
  detect that unwanted low-pass contribution.
- [WaveletDecomposition.cs](../sources/Wavelet/WaveletDecomposition.cs) uses
  double products and accumulators in real and complex convolutions. This
  reduces cancellation in multilevel biorthogonal reconstruction. Stored bands
  and public outputs remain single precision. Matrices now reject a dimension
  that becomes odd at an active level, matching the vector overload contract.
- [Meyer.cs](../sources/Wavelet/Meyer.cs) evaluates all removable singularities:
  scaling at zero and +/-3/4, wavelet at 1/2 and 1/2 +/-3/4 or +/-3/8. Double
  intermediates also stabilize adjacent float arguments.

[WaveletRepairTests.cs](../tests/UMapx.Tests/WaveletRepairTests.cs) covers all 28
Bior/CDF properties, both normalization modes, every impulse position at lengths
8/32/128, complex vectors and rectangular matrices, DC rejection and vanishing
moments, and invalid multilevel shapes. Meyer references come from independent
frequency-domain quadrature, including neighboring float values around its
singularities. Biorthogonal cancellation was cross-checked against the filter
construction in the [PyWavelets source](https://github.com/PyWavelets/pywt/blob/main/pywt/_extensions/c/wavelets.c).

## B08 — windows

[BartlettHann.cs](../sources/Window/BartlettHann.cs) corrects the cosine sign.
[Normal.cs](../sources/Window/Normal.cs) and
[Confined.cs](../sources/Window/Confined.cs) use the fractional center (N-1)/2.
Confined uses the requested frame size throughout, rather than mixing it with
the stored size. Its symmetric Gaussian ratio is evaluated without dividing two
underflowed boundary values. All three windows return the unit singleton window.

[WindowRepairTests.cs](../tests/UMapx.Tests/WindowRepairTests.cs) checks independent
formulas, explicit/stored sizes, symmetry, singleton behavior, and narrow widths
for lengths 1, 2, 3, 8, 9, 32, and 65.

## B09 — transforms and response filters

- [HankelTransform.cs](../sources/Transform/HankelTransform.cs) brackets consecutive
  positive Bessel zeros, starting above the order, and bisects each bracket.
  It cannot converge to the origin as the old unguarded Newton search did.
  The scan is bounded and reports failure if distinct roots cannot be located.
  Root ordering follows the [NIST Bessel-zero properties](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.21).
- [LaplacianPyramidTransform.cs](../sources/Transform/LaplacianPyramidTransform.cs)
  starts vector reconstruction at the last valid pyramid index. Singleton and
  thin inputs retain a base level.
- [BilateralGridFilter.cs](../sources/Transform/BilateralGridFilter.cs) uses
  magnitude for range guidance and accumulates the full complex sample. The
  same weights act on both components, preserving phase rotations and
  conjugation. The existing unit-interval magnitude domain is documented.
- [LocalLaplacianFilter.cs](../sources/Transform/LocalLaplacianFilter.cs) modifies
  detail bands and preserves the coarsest Gaussian level. Its lookup tables
  include both endpoints and interpolate neighboring samples. Integer sample
  indices avoid cumulative step error. Zero width, zero intervals, and inputs
  with no detail levels produce the identity. This detail/base separation agrees
  with the [authors' local-Laplacian formulation](https://people.csail.mit.edu/hasinoff/pubs/AubryEtAl14-lapfilters.pdf).
- [ThresholdFilter.cs](../sources/Transform/ThresholdFilter.cs) applies Under/Over
  independently to the real and imaginary parts for matrices as already done
  for vectors. Abs uses magnitude and removes the whole sample. Equality is retained.
- [IIR.cs](../sources/Response/IIR.cs) tests the denominator actually used by
  Reaction: `(1-a[0])*z^(Q-1) - a[1]*z^(Q-2) - ... - a[Q-1]`.
  Real Schur recursion tests strict stability without computing roots.
  Undefined feedback (`a[0] == 1`) and nonfinite coefficients return false.
- [ConeShape.cs](../sources/Distribution/ConeShape.cs) uses `1/abs(tau)` in the
  time kernel, giving the correct nonnegative mass for either sign of the lag.

[TransformFilterRepairTests.cs](../tests/UMapx.Tests/TransformFilterRepairTests.cs)
checks Hankel matrices against 70-digit references at orders 0, 1, 5, 10, 20, and
50; known real, complex-conjugate, and repeated IIR poles; complex reconstruction;
phase/conjugation invariance; and threshold components. It independently sums
bilateral-grid sample influences and compares local-Laplacian output against
direct double remapping and explicit pyramids without lookup tables. The latter
has a stated 3e-4 interpolation budget for the tested widths. Cone kernel mass
is checked by numerical integration.

## B10 — color spaces

[XYZ.cs](../sources/Colorspace/XYZ.cs) preserves nonnegative tristimulus values
above one, including the library's D65 Z coordinate 1.089. This also repairs
downstream Lab conversion. The CIE piecewise function uses exact rational
threshold/slope constants, consistent with the existing Lab inverse. The
library's four-decimal sRGB matrix and white-point constants are retained;
they are not silently replaced by a different precision convention. The
relative-coordinate and piecewise definitions can be compared with the
[W3C color-conversion reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#color-conversion-code).

[RYB.cs](../sources/Colorspace/RYB.cs) avoids zero normalization for neutral colors
and rounds intermediate/output bytes to reduce chroma bias.
[AHSL.cs](../sources/Colorspace/AHSL.cs) derives saturation from full chroma,
avoiding the false zero when only the red component equals gray.

[ColorSpaceRepairTests.cs](../tests/UMapx.Tests/ColorSpaceRepairTests.cs) checks all
neutral bytes, 1,024 deterministic colors per repaired model, chromatic inputs
with red equal to gray, XYZ values above one, and both sides of the CIE Lab
piecewise threshold. The round-trip budget of two intensity levels per channel is unchanged.
